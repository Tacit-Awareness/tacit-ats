# tATS prototype

A design shell for the client side of tATS: the place a client's hiring team checks where every applicant stands, reads interviews, and steps in only when something needs them. Grokbots (one per interviewer, working as that interviewer's assistant) do the asking, scheduling and chasing. It is a single static page with no backend. Nothing here is wired to `apps/web` yet.

Meridian is the example client. Every applicant, interviewer, run and transcript is placeholder data.

## Run it

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4480 --directory design/tats
```

Then open http://localhost:4480. GSAP, Flip and morphdom load from public CDNs, so it needs a network connection.

To change it, edit `src/` and run `python3 design/tats/build.py`, which inlines `src/` into `index.html`.

## What's in it

The sidebar follows the funnel: Home, Inbox and CRM, then **Top of Funnel** (Job Postings, Applicant Tickets, Analytics), then **Routing & Tracking** (Pipeline, Interviews, Calendar, Interviewers, Team setup, Referrals, Grokbot Activity). Each of the four daily pages has its own shape, so you can tell them apart before reading a row:

No summary rows or banners: every page opens straight on its content. The palette is Linear's: Mercury White (#F4F5F8) frame, white panels and boxes with one shared soft elevation, Nordic Gray (#222326) ink, reading panes one step back; in dark mode Nordic Gray is the surface. Structure is square (panels, cards, tables, inputs, pop-ups) and anything you press is a pill; every set of options sits in one grey pill track with the picked one dark. Header strips and table heads are shaded one step down. Motion is 0.2s or less with a slight spring, pages arrive whole with no staggered entrances, and the tutorial moves in the same click. Color is grey except one accent for the primary action, red for what's broken, and the three interview verdicts. Rows and cards carry no buttons; a reader has one action, and anything else goes to the Grokbot through "Ask your Grokbot" or ⌘K, where open issues show up as one-line commands.

- **Home** (`#home`, the default): Recruiting Overview, built around "where are candidates getting stuck?". Filters for period, team and role. The slider (Overview, Applicant Tickets, Interviews, Referrals) switches tabs inside Home (`#home~tickets`, `#home~interviews`, `#home~referrals`); it stays at the top, so Overview is always one click back.
  - KPI row: New applications (daily, against the period before), Candidates in process (by stage; opens the CRM filtered to active), Time to review applications, Time to schedule, Time to submit interviewer feedback (each a chart of how long open items have waited), and Interview workload (team hours booked or awaiting feedback, by team). Workload carries a dashed "Estimate" callout for Testbench replacing first-round live technicals (30 to 60 minutes each), kept apart from actual hours. Targets are user-set; none are assumed.
  - "Where are candidates getting stuck?": Bottlenecks beside Time in stage. Stages use the company's interview process, set and renamed in Settings.
  - Secondary grid: Time to submit by interviewer, Interview efficiency (round pass-through, interviews and interviewer hours per hire, from closed applications), Outstanding feedback. Interview outcomes and Offer approvals are left out until confirmed.
  - Compare styles (`#compare`): the same layout in the current styling and in the previous palette with refined shading, side by side; either can be applied everywhere (`?pal=prev` also works).
- **Inbox** (`#inbox`): a digest, not an email list. One card, "Welcome back · N worth your time, from M updates", over the last 1 day, 3 days or 1 week. **Needs you** first (a Grokbot that broke, an invite drafted but not sent, overdue feedback or Testbench, a decision that's yours), then **Decisions made**, **Booked** and **FYI**, each a one-line sentence with who's involved, where it came from and when. Opening it, the Grokbot reads each source (Slack, Gmail, Calendar, Grokbots) and ticks it off, then the rows fill in one at a time; it takes about two seconds, and Stop or a click skips it. Clicking a problem opens a pop-up with the fix already drafted by the Grokbot (the brief by email, the invite from the interviewer's Gmail, a Slack nudge, a reminder to the candidate), editable, with one Send. A decision that's yours, and every row under Decisions made, Booked and FYI, opens the whole ticket in a pop-up that fills the page: the loop path, the interviewer's read with Next round, Move to offer and Reject (A, O, R), Testbench, profile and activity. ✕, Esc or a click outside closes it back to the Inbox; "Open as a page" goes to the ticket. ↗ opens the ticket, ✓ dismisses or marks read, and Mark all as read clears everything but Needs you.
- **Applicant Tickets** (`#triage`): new, unrouted applicants in an email-style list; the reader shows their whole application as one document, with a control panel below (next step as large pills, the team, the drafted reply as an editable preview, and a large Send); the picked move (Interview, Testbench or Decline) carries the same highlight as hover. Tickets are grouped by team.
- **CRM** (`#crm`): one database of every candidate: those in process and everyone the funnel closed, hired or not. A table (candidate, role, profile, owner, Testbench, outcome, last contact) with views for All, In process, Hired, Silver medalists, Rejected and Withdrew, sortable columns, 50 per page, and search. "Ask Grokbot for a list" takes plain words ("silver medalists on infra, Testbench over 85") or the selected rows and shows the Slack post its Grokbot will send, channel included.
- **Pipeline** (`#all`): every open application, as a board by stage (New, Testbench, With Grokbots, Interview booked, Your call); columns show 12 and "Show all", the list layout shows 25 per group. Search says how many past candidates in the CRM also match. A returning candidate is labelled "Applied before" and their ticket lists past applications.
- **Scale test data**: about 260 generated active applicants and 2,400 past applications (seeded, the same on every load). They live only in Pipeline and the CRM, so the Inbox, Tickets and Interviews keep the hand-written examples.
- **Interviews** (`#interviews`): feedback and sentiment. A bar of how interviews went, the list grouped Pass, Mixed, Fail, Read pending, and a reader that opens with the candidate's loop as a path read left to right (each round by team with who ran it and their verdict, the one you're reading outlined, the next step marked Up next with who the Grokbot will ask, the rest faded, ending at the offer, and how many rounds are left), then the interviewer's read, their summary and next steps. Check each one off. (Granola notes, questions and answers, and transcripts come back once an interviewer connects Granola.)
- **Referrals** (`#referrals`): every referral (open and closed) with who referred them and where they are now, plus top referrers by hires. "Add referral" opens a form that creates an Applicant Ticket sourced to the referrer; "Add with Grokbot" takes a pasted Slack message, email or link and fills the form. Typing "refer …" in ⌘K or the chat does the same.
- **Grokbot Activity** (`#activity`): "Needs you" first (only failed or stuck tasks, each with its fix), then a searchable log.
- **Job Postings** and **Analytics**: the careers page as applicants see it (see `design/careers`), and how postings perform.
- **Chat with your Grokbot** (`#chat`, from ⌘K): the same DM as in Slack. tATS is Slack-native; this dashboard is for checking in and delegating. The Grokbot answers "what needs me", "who owes a scorecard", "nudge Daniel", "send the invite", "silver medalists on infra" and referrals, with Slack-style buttons.
- **Team setup** (`#setup`, from Interviewers › Manage people & teams, Settings, or ⌘K): onboarding and the ongoing people-and-teams portal, in four steps with a setup checklist beside them. People & Grokbots (add someone or import from Slack; their Grokbot invite goes out in Slack; turn a Grokbot on or off). Teams (a matrix of people by team; tick more than one team to make someone multidisciplinary; pick each team's lead; add a team). Interview skills (skill tags such as GPU performance or FDE, and the interview types each person runs; star one to prioritize them for it). Review (who covers each interview type, prioritized people ringed, gaps flagged). The Grokbots route from this: people without a connected Grokbot are skipped, and a starred interview type outweighs an unstarred one when picking who interviews.
- **Tutorial** (bottom of the sidebar): a twelve-step walkthrough that moves across the pages, spotlighting each part and shading the rest. Arrow keys or Next/Back; Esc ends it.
- **Account** (bottom of the sidebar): just your name, no picture. It opens a menu with who you are (role and email), Team setup, Settings (⌘ ,), Tutorial, a Light / Dark / System theme switch, and Sign out. Team setup also has its own place in the sidebar, so it isn't buried in Settings.
- The sidebar resizes by dragging its edge and collapses with `[`. Routing rules live in Settings.
