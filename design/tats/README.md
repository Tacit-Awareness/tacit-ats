# tATS prototype

A design shell for the client side of tATS: the place a client's hiring team checks where every applicant stands, reads interviews, and steps in only when something needs them. Grokbots (one per interviewer, working as that interviewer's assistant) do the asking, scheduling and chasing. It is a single static page with no backend. Nothing here is wired to `apps/web` yet.

Meridian is the example client. Every applicant, interviewer, run and transcript is placeholder data.

## Run it

```bash
python3 -m http.server 4480 --directory design/tats
```

Then open http://localhost:4480. GSAP, Flip and morphdom load from public CDNs, so it needs a network connection.

To change it, edit `src/` and run `python3 design/tats/build.py`, which inlines `src/` into `index.html`.

## What's in it

The sidebar follows the funnel: Home and Inbox, then **Top of Funnel** (Job Postings, New Applicants, Analytics), then **Routing & Tracking** (Applicant Tickets, Interview Judgement, Interview Calendar, Referrals), then **Onboarding** (Team & Grokbots, Interviewers, Interview process, CRM). Each of the four daily pages has its own shape, so you can tell them apart before reading a row:

No summary rows or banners: every page opens straight on its content. The palette is Linear's layout with brand-tinted neutrals: one scale (`--n0` to `--n1000`) from near-white to near-black, every step carrying the brand's hue at Linear's whisper of chroma (~0.005, hue 271), used for every frame, panel, line, shadow and text colour instead of pure greys, so it reads as cool white and graphite rather than blue. Light lives on the 1 / 3 / 5 / 10% steps (the frame and sidebar are 3%, #F4F5F8 is 5%), with text on 70 / 80 / 90% (#222326 is 90%); dark mode mirrors it with #222326 as the panel. `--brand-h` retints it and `--tint` sets how strong. The page you're on is a solid ink block in the sidebar with paper text. Panels, cards, inputs and pop-ups are rounded, and anything you press is a pill; every set of options sits in one grey pill track with the picked one dark. Header strips and table heads are shaded one step down. Type is Mona Sans (variable, inlined from `src/MonaSans.woff2`, a Latin subset; licence in `src/MonaSans-OFL.txt`) on one scale, 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 16 / 18 / 20 / 22 / 24 / 28 px and up, each size with its own tracking: small sizes open up (+0.006em at 12px), titles close in (-0.016em at 28px). Every padding, gap and margin sits on 0 / 1 / 2 / 4 / 6 / 8 / 10 / 12 / 16 / 20 / 24 / 28 / 32 / 40 / 48 / 56 / 64. Motion follows raphaelsalaja/skills: hover colours change at once, presses scale to 0.98, anything that appears eases out on cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1) and anything that moves on (0.77, 0, 0.175, 1), with no overshoot; closes run shorter than opens, nothing grows from below 0.95, menus grow from their trigger, swapped icons cross-fade with a blur, the palette opened from the keyboard appears at once, and a theme switch repaints in one frame. Motion is 0.2s or less; hovering any pill group slides one grey glass pill under the option you point at, pages arrive whole with no staggered entrances, and the tutorial moves in the same click. Color is the tinted neutral scale except one accent for the primary action, red for what's broken, and the three interview verdicts. Rows and cards carry no buttons; a reader has one action, and anything else goes to the Grokbot through "Ask your Grokbot" or ⌘K, where open issues show up as one-line commands.

- **Home** (`#home`, the default): Recruiting Overview, built around "where are candidates getting stuck?". Everything below is a widget you can move, resize, remove or add back. Filters for period, team and role. The slider (Overview, Applicant Tickets, Interviews, Referrals) switches tabs inside Home (`#home~tickets`, `#home~interviews`, `#home~referrals`); it stays at the top, so Overview is always one click back.
  - KPI row: New applications (daily, against the period before), Candidates in process (by stage; opens the CRM filtered to active), Time to review applications, Time to schedule, Time to submit interviewer feedback (each a chart of how long open items have waited), and Interview workload (team hours booked or awaiting feedback, by team). Workload carries a dashed "Estimate" callout for Testbench replacing first-round live technicals (30 to 60 minutes each), kept apart from actual hours. Targets are user-set; none are assumed.
  - "Where are candidates getting stuck?": Bottlenecks beside Time in stage. Stages are the steps of the company's interview process, in order, as drawn in Onboarding's flowchart (steps side by side after a split count as one stage); rename a step there and Home, the CRM and job postings follow. "Edit stages" opens the flowchart.
  - Secondary grid: Time to submit by interviewer, Interview efficiency (round pass-through, interviews and interviewer hours per hire, from closed applications), Outstanding feedback. Interview outcomes and Offer approvals are left out until confirmed.
  - **Widgets**: the Overview is a grid of widgets in the style of a modern analytics dashboard: rounded white panels on the 3% ground, the number first with a quiet label under it, the change (green ▲ / red ▼) or the count on the right, then one chart: a blue line with a fade under it and a dot on today, soft grey bars, or a split bar with a legend. "Edit widgets" (where Compare styles was) works like the iPhone home screen: drag a widget to move it and the others slide out of its way, − removes it, its size pill cycles Small / Wide / Full, and "Add widget" (or the dashed tile) opens a gallery of every block with a live preview: the six KPIs, Applications over time (this period against the last), Hires, Where applicants come from, Grokbots connected, Offers awaiting approval, Bottlenecks, Time in stage, Time to submit by interviewer, Interview efficiency and Outstanding feedback. Done or Esc ends it. The Overview fills one screen and never scrolls: rows share the window's height and each widget drops detail as its cell gets shorter (extra legend rows and the estimate, then the footer and axis). The default is the six KPIs, Applications over time and Hires, three by three; "Present" takes it full screen without the sidebar, tabs or toasts, for a projector (Esc to leave). The layout is saved in the browser (`tats.widgets.v2`); "Reset to the default layout" is at the foot of the gallery. `WIDGETS` in `app.js`.
  - Compare styles (`#compare`) still opens by its link: the same layout in the current styling and in the previous palette, side by side; either can be applied everywhere (`?pal=prev` also works).
- **Inbox** (`#inbox`): a digest, not an email list. One card, "Welcome back · N worth your time, from M updates", over the last 1 day, 3 days or 1 week. **Needs you** first (a Grokbot that broke, an invite drafted but not sent, overdue feedback or Testbench, a decision that's yours), then **Decisions made**, **Booked** and **FYI**, each a one-line sentence with who's involved, where it came from and when. Opening it, the Grokbot reads each source (Slack, Gmail, Calendar, Grokbots) and ticks it off, then the rows fill in one at a time; it takes about two seconds, and Stop or a click skips it. Clicking a problem opens a pop-up with the fix already drafted by the Grokbot (the brief by email, the invite from the interviewer's Gmail, a Slack nudge, a reminder to the candidate), editable, with one Send. A decision that's yours, and every row under Decisions made, Booked and FYI, opens the whole ticket in a pop-up that fills the page: the loop path, the interviewer's read with Next round, Move to offer and Reject (A, O, R), Testbench, profile and activity. ✕, Esc or a click outside closes it back to the Inbox; "Open as a page" goes to the ticket. ↗ opens the ticket, ✓ dismisses or marks read, and Mark all as read clears everything but Needs you.
- **New Applicants** (`#triage`): new, unrouted applicants in an email-style list; the reader shows their whole application as one document, with a control panel below (next step as large pills, the team, the drafted reply as an editable preview, and a large Send); the picked move (Interview, Testbench or Decline) carries the same highlight as hover. Tickets are grouped by team.
- **CRM** (`#crm`): one database of every candidate: those in process and everyone the funnel closed, hired or not. A table (candidate, role, profile, owner, Testbench, outcome, last contact) with views for All, In process, Hired, Silver medalists, Rejected and Withdrew, sortable columns, 50 per page, and search. "Ask Grokbot for a list" takes plain words ("silver medalists on infra, Testbench over 85") or the selected rows and shows the Slack post its Grokbot will send, channel included.
- **Banners** (`#banners`, the Banner playground, from ⌘K): a status message after Fluid Functionalism's Banner. Five statuses (default, info, success, warning, error), low or high contrast, inline or fixed (full-bleed, stuck to the top of its scroller), default or compact, up to three actions laid out primary, secondary, ghost, and a ✕ that folds its height away (dismissed for good once it has an id). The playground has live controls, a preview page, and every status in both contrasts. In use: an info banner on Interview Calendar for who's away. No reminder banners (counts of stuck tickets and the like): those live on the pages themselves. `banner()` in `app.js`.
- **Applicant Tickets** (`#all`, formerly Pipeline): built for hundreds of people. It opens on **Stuck**, only those past their stage's target (New 1 day, Testbench 3 days, With Grokbots 3 days, Your call 2 days; a booked interview still ahead is never stuck). **Everyone** shows the whole pipeline, 12 per stage with "Show all". A stage bar across the top sizes each stage by headcount, shows how many are stuck with a red share, and filters when clicked. Below it, one line per person reads like an email: name, team tag, what's happening in bold ("Waiting on Daniel's read"), the facts that matter in grey (role, round, Testbench, source), the next move as a tag ("Nudge Daniel", "Decide") and time in stage, red once it's late. Rows are grouped by stage and open the full-page pop-up. The board appears only when one role is picked, where it fits on a screen. Search says how many past candidates in the CRM also match. A returning candidate is labelled "Applied before" and their ticket lists past applications.
  - **A ticket** (`#t~APP-…`, or the pop-up from any list) opens on **where it stands**: who has it, what they're waiting on, the time in the stage against its target ("Stuck · 22h over" in red past it), then "To move it on" with the one button that does it (Nudge Marcus, Remind Inez, Send the invite, Pick the interviewer). Nudging doesn't reset the stage clock. Under the profile, **Application** shows the same pages New Applicants reads (resume, research statement, note, Testbench report), one at a time. Teams wear an icon (Research a flask, Engineering a server stack, Product a layout) instead of a letter, and the Team chip is a quick menu.
- **Scale test data**: about 260 generated active applicants and 2,400 past applications (seeded, the same on every load). They live only in Applicant Tickets and the CRM, so the Inbox, New Applicants and Interview Judgement keep the hand-written examples.
- **Interview Judgement** (`#interviews`): feedback and sentiment. A bar of how interviews went, the list grouped Pass, Mixed, Fail, Read pending, and a reader that opens with the candidate's routing graph, drawn from the company's interview process: Applied, the split by team with their branch lit and the other teams faded, each round with who ran it and their verdict, the one you're reading outlined, the next step marked Up next with who the Grokbot will ask, and the Offer, plus how many rounds are left. Click the graph to open the process as an editable flowchart over the page. Then the interviewer's read, their summary and next steps; check each one off. (Granola notes, questions and answers, and transcripts come back once an interviewer connects Granola.)
- **Referrals** (`#referrals`): every referral (open and closed) with who referred them and where they are now, plus top referrers by hires. "Add referral" opens a form that creates an Applicant Ticket sourced to the referrer; "Add with Grokbot" takes a pasted Slack message, email or link and fills the form. Typing "refer …" in ⌘K or the chat does the same.
- **Grokbot runs**: there is no activity page. A Grokbot task that fails or gets stuck comes to the Inbox under Needs you, with its fix; "View run" on a ticket opens that run over the ticket (`#run~R-401`). Old `#activity` links land in the Inbox.
- **Job Postings** and **Analytics**: the careers page as applicants see it (see `design/careers`), and how postings perform.
- **Chat with your Grokbot** (`#chat`, from ⌘K): the same DM as in Slack. tATS is Slack-native; this dashboard is for checking in and delegating. The Grokbot answers "what needs me", "who owes a scorecard", "nudge Daniel", "send the invite", "silver medalists on infra" and referrals, with Slack-style buttons.
- **Onboarding** (`#setup`, its own sidebar section; also in the account menu and ⌘K): first-run setup and the ongoing portal, in five steps with a checklist beside them. People & Grokbots (add someone or import from Slack; their Grokbot invite goes out in Slack; turn a Grokbot on or off). Teams (a matrix of people by team; tick more than one team to make someone multidisciplinary; pick each team's lead). Interview skills (skill tags such as GPU performance or FDE, and the interview types each person runs; star one to prioritize them). **Interview process** (`#setup~process`): a flowchart canvas. Drag steps, drag from a step's dot to connect, double-click to add a step, add splits and sticky notes, Delete removes, ⌘Z undoes, scroll to pan and ⌘ + scroll to zoom. Click a step to name it, say what kind it is (review, Testbench, interview), who runs it (you, their team, another team, a specific team or pinned people) and how long. There is no chat box on the canvas: the Grokbot changes the process from Slack or the CLI (and the chat here, the same DM), e.g. "add a references check before the offer in the process", "remove the cross-team interview from the process", or a whole description, and offers "Open the process". Review (who covers each interview type, gaps flagged). The page is live with your Grokbot: ask it in Slack or the CLI ("put Daniel Okafor on Engineering, Product and Research", "star Sam for Systems design", "turn off Wei's Grokbot", "tag Noor with CUDA", "add Jane Doe as staff engineer") and its cursor comes onto the page with a name tag saying what it's doing, goes to each box and makes the change. The Grokbot doesn't live on the page: the subtitle points to Slack and the CLI, which open as their own windows (`?surface=slack`, `?surface=cli`, a Slack DM and a terminal). Type a request there and the open tATS window does it with the Grokbot's cursor, then the answer comes back in Slack or the terminal (they talk over a BroadcastChannel, so keep both open in the same browser). General questions ("what needs me") are answered there too. The Grokbots route from this: they follow each candidate's branch to know who interviews next, skip people without a connected Grokbot, and prefer starred interview types.
- **Tutorial** (bottom of the sidebar): an eleven-step walkthrough that moves across the pages, spotlighting each part and shading the rest. Arrow keys or Next/Back; Esc ends it.
- **Account** (bottom of the sidebar): just your name, no picture. It opens a menu with who you are (role and email), Team setup, Settings (⌘ ,), Tutorial, a Light / Dark / System theme switch, and Sign out. Team setup also has its own place in the sidebar, so it isn't buried in Settings.
- The sidebar resizes by dragging its edge and collapses to an icon rail with `[`; drag the rail's edge back out to open it. Collapsing eases (0.26s, no spring): the edge cuts the labels to "…" as it passes, and `transition-behavior: allow-discrete` lets them leave the layout only once the rail arrives. A section's ▾ folds its rows the same way. Its spacing (row height, gaps between rows and sections) follows the window's height in three tiers. Routing rules live in Settings.
- **UI scale**: the layout is drawn for a ~1440 × 840 window. Larger windows zoom the whole interface by the smaller of the width and height ratios, up to 1.6×, so pages fill big displays instead of floating small. Every `vh` goes through `--vh` so it stays true under the zoom, and a shim at the top of `app.js` hands rects, pointer positions and the window size back in CSS pixels, so drags and pop-ups need no changes. `UI.base` and `UI.max` tune it.
- Applicant Tickets' stage cards flag what's past target as a soft red "N stuck" pill.
- **Warmth fix** (account menu): Night Shift and True Tone warm the screen after the page draws, and no browser API reports them, so each display is corrected once by eye (Off, 1 to 4: red and a little green pulled down until whites look neutral again). tATS remembers the level per display (size, pixel density, colour range) and separately for day and night (7pm to 7am), and reapplies it by itself when the window moves to another screen or evening starts. Off draws no filter, so the colours are exact. `WB` in `app.js`.
