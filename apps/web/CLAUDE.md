# @tacit-ats/web

Next.js (App Router): the client area, where members of a client org sign in and work on their own jobs and pipeline.

Where members of a client org sign in and work: Phase 1 of Tacit-Awareness's `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-ats-parity-design.md`. Dormant until configured: `/client/**` answers 404 unless `WORKOS_CLIENT_ID`, `WORKOS_API_KEY`, `WORKOS_COOKIE_PASSWORD` (32+ characters), `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` are all set (`lib/client/config.ts`; the one-time WorkOS and Supabase steps are in this repo's `.env.example`).

- **One session, under one prefix** (`proxy.ts`, which matches only `/client` and `/client/**`; `/` redirects to `/client`). Clients sign in with WorkOS AuthKit (`wos-session`). Until 2026-10-14 this area lived inside Tacit-Awareness's `apps/internal`, split by path from the staff session; it moved here so the client product and Tacit's workspace no longer share an app, a domain or a session.
- **`lib/client/session.ts`**: `resolveClient()` builds a per-request database client from the user's own WorkOS access token (`createUserClient`, role `authenticated`) and asks the database who that is (`current_actor_id()`), so the page and the policies agree by construction. Not bound yet means a first sign-in: `lib/client/bind.ts` matches the verified email to the actor an admin invited, inside the token's org (`@tacit-ats/core`'s `bindClientActor`). Nobody is created on sign-in. `requireClient()` is the throwing form, for actions.
- **Sign-ins that start at WorkOS.** Accepting a WorkOS invitation (or signing up from its email) runs AuthKit's hosted flow and then lands on `/client/callback` without the PKCE cookie our own sign-in sets, so AuthKit cannot verify it and refuses (`CallbackError` `missing_pkce_cookie` / `missing_auth_params` / `oauth_state_mismatch`). The account exists by then; the callback's `onError` (`lib/client/callback-error.ts`) answers with a "Your account is ready, continue" page linking to `/client`, which signs in from our side. Any other failure is a plain "Couldn't sign in, try again" page, never the error itself. A link, not a redirect, so a browser that drops the cookie cannot loop. (Until 2026-10-14 this was AuthKit's bare JSON error, the first thing an invited client saw.)
- **`lib/client/bind.ts` is the only file in the client area that may touch the service role**, and it returns a verdict, never the client.
- **`src/client-area.test.ts`** enforces the rules above from the source: the import walk (nothing reachable from `app/client` or `lib/client` may name a privileged client, except `lib/client/bind.ts`), and that every server action starts with `await requireClient()`.
- **Pages so far**: `/client` (org, your level, the jobs you can see, the candidate cards a limited interviewer gets, the team), `/client/jobs` (the jobs you can see, with pipeline counts unless you are an interviewer) and `/client/jobs/[id]` (2026-10-13: the job and its pipeline on the shared `components/pipeline-board.tsx`, plus "Add a candidate"). Everything is read through RLS; nothing is filtered in the page. A refused sign-in renders `app/client/refusal.tsx` on every page.
- **The job board** reads with core's `getRolePipeline` on the member's own client, so the cards are exactly the applications they may read (an external recruiter sees only their own adds; an interviewer gets a pointer to `/client` instead of a board). The edit controls appear only when `can_on_role(job, 'can_edit_pipeline')` says so, and "Add a candidate" also needs `can_add_people()`. The writes (`jobs/[id]/actions.ts`: add, move, edit stage/owner/next action, log a touch, remove) are core's pipeline functions on the same client, so the policies decide again. Adding goes through core's `addCandidateToRole`: an email already on a person the member can see reuses that person, otherwise a people row is made in the org; a person in the org the member cannot see is not revealed, so they get a second row. The action checks `can_edit_pipeline` before creating anyone, so a refused add leaves no orphan person. Notices and errors are codes (`jobs/messages.ts`). Core never touches the Tacit funnel for a client org's application (`advancePersonFunnel` returns for any org but Tacit's), and `getRolePipeline`'s `companyName` is empty for a member who may not read `companies` (everyone but an admin). Cards do not link anywhere yet: there is no client person page.
- **Where a client org starts**: Tacit-Awareness's staff `/orgs` page (its `apps/internal`), with `@tacit/core`'s `createClientOrg` and `inviteOrgMember` on the service role. Nothing in this repo creates an org or its first admin; a first sign-in here only binds the actor that page invited.
- **`/client/team`** (2026-10-14): the org's members for everyone; an admin also sees who is invited and not signed in yet, and invites, re-sends and withdraws (`team/actions.ts`, codes in `team/messages.ts`). No service role: the actor is written by the `invite_org_member` definer function on the admin's own token (Tacit-Awareness's packages/db, `20261014120000_client_invites`), which checks the caller is an admin and writes only into the org on that token, so the form never names an org. WorkOS then sends the email (`lib/client/workos.ts`, AuthKit's own `getWorkOS()` client, with the inviter named), and a first invitation WorkOS refuses is withdrawn again. Withdrawing deletes the unbound actor and revokes WorkOS's pending invitation for the address, best-effort: an accepted email with no actor behind it binds nobody. `@tacit-ats/core`'s `client-auth/invite.ts` holds the sequence. `ClientContext` now carries the token's WorkOS organization and user ids for this.
- Not built yet: a client person page (cards are plain names), creating and editing jobs, changing a member's access level, files. The WorkOS and Supabase setup was done on 2026-10-13; production is `ats.tacitawareness.com`, where a test client org's admin first signed in on 2026-10-15.
- **`/client/calendar`** (2026-10-17): a member connects their own Google Calendar for the client connector's scheduling (apps/mcp `find_interview_times`, `schedule_interview`). `calendar/connect` (GET, `resolveClient()`) sets a 10-minute httpOnly state cookie bound to the actor and redirects to Google's consent (`lib/client/google-calendar.ts`: scopes openid, email, `calendar.freebusy`, `calendar.events`, offline, `prompt=consent`); `calendar/callback` checks the state against the cookie, exchanges the code (refusing a grant without both calendar scopes, a refresh token or a verified account email), and stores it with `client_connect_calendar` on the member's own token, which writes only the caller's row (Tacit-Awareness's packages/db, `20261017120000_client_calendar_connections`). The page reads the member's own row (never the refresh token, which `authenticated` is not granted); `calendar/actions.ts` disconnects through `client_disconnect_calendar`. Codes in `calendar/messages.ts`. Needs `CLIENT_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` / `CLIENT_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `<origin>/client/calendar/callback` registered with Google; unset, the page says it is not set up. Linked from the client header as "Calendar".
- **`/client/docs`** (2026-10-14): the client connector's docs, linked from the client header as "Documentation", behind the same `resolveClient()` gate as the other client pages. The pages are data in `lib/docs/client-pages.ts`, rendered by `components/docs/docs-view.tsx` (react-markdown + remark-gfm) with a ⌘K search. `lib/docs/docs.test.ts` reads `apps/mcp/src/client/server.ts` and fails when the tool reference lists a tool that is not registered or misses one that is: **add a row to the reference page when you add a tool.** Tacit-Awareness's staff `/docs` shows a copy of these pages (its `lib/docs/client-pages.ts`); this file is the source, so tell that repo when it changes. `CLIENT_CONNECTOR_URL` is `https://ats-mcp.tacitawareness.com/api/client`, the mcp app's production domain: it must equal that project's `CLIENT_MCP_RESOURCE_URL`, the token audience.
- **Public job posts** (`/jobs/[slug]`, `/api/jobs/[slug]/apply`, 2026-10-16): a client's live post and its application form, for anyone with the link. Not part of the client area and not behind any session: `proxy.ts` runs only under `/client` (pinned by `proxy.test.ts`'s matcher test). The only database access is `lib/public/jobs.ts`, on the publishable key with no token (`createUserClient` with a null token, which also refuses a secret key), so every call is `anon`, which may run `job_post_public` and `submit_job_application` and read nothing else (Tacit-Awareness's packages/db, `20261016150000_client_job_posts`). A wrong slug, a draft, a closed post and a post on a job that is not open are the same 404. The page is server-rendered with no script: one input per question named `a_<question id>`, the browser's own `required` / `maxLength` / `type` checks first, and a hidden `website` honeypot the database answers with a quiet success. The form posts to the route, which calls the function and answers with a 303 back to the page carrying a code (`applied=1`, or `error=required|too_long|invalid|busy|failed` and `q=<question id>`), never the database's message; the page owns the wording. What was typed is not kept across an error redirect, which is why the browser checks matter. No resume upload yet: the resume question takes a link. `client-area.test.ts` walks the imports from `app/jobs`, `app/api/jobs` and `lib/public` and fails on a session, a privileged client or a `"use server"` file. The members' side (posting, publishing, the applicants inbox) is apps/mcp's client connector. Since 2026-10-16 the form ends with a **voluntary self-identification** fieldset (US EEO-1 gender and race / ethnicity, veteran and disability status; `EEOC_QUESTIONS` in `lib/public/jobs.ts`), apart from the post's questions, every select defaulting to "I decline to self-identify" and saying the hiring team never sees the answers; the fields are `eeoc_<question>`, passed as `submit_job_application`'s `p_eeoc` and stored with source `candidate` on the new application (Tacit-Awareness's packages/db, `20261016170000_client_reports_eeoc`). Members only ever see an admin's suppressed totals (apps/mcp's `eeoc_report`).

## Shared pieces

- `components/pipeline-board.tsx`, `components/submit-button.tsx`, `components/copy-button.tsx`, `components/docs/*`, `components/ui/*`, `lib/utils.ts` and `app/globals.css` started as copies of Tacit-Awareness's `apps/internal` ones and since 2026-10-15 follow Tacit Testbench's skin instead (its `apps/web` `app/skin.css` `.tb-skin`: Neco / Recoleta / Geist Mono, frosted `glass` cards, pill buttons, a near-black primary and one link blue; the fonts are self-hosted from `public/fonts`). `globals.css` holds the tokens and the `glass`, `field`, `notice` and `inline-link` utilities the pages use. They are not synced; change them here freely.
- Everything the pages read or write goes through `@tacit/core`'s pipeline functions (vendored, `vendor/`) or `@tacit-ats/core`, always on the member's own client.
