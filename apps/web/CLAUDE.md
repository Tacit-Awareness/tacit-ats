# @tacit-ats/web

Next.js (App Router): the client area, where members of a client org sign in and work on their own jobs and pipeline.

Where members of a client org sign in and work: Phase 1 of tacit-internal's `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-13-ats-parity-design.md`. Dormant until configured: `/client/**` answers 404 unless `WORKOS_CLIENT_ID`, `WORKOS_API_KEY`, `WORKOS_COOKIE_PASSWORD` (32+ characters), `SUPABASE_URL` and `SUPABASE_PUBLISHABLE_KEY` are all set (`lib/client/config.ts`; the one-time WorkOS and Supabase steps are in this repo's `.env.example`).

- **One session, under one prefix** (`proxy.ts`, which matches only `/client` and `/client/**`; `/` redirects to `/client`). Clients sign in with WorkOS AuthKit (`wos-session`). Until 2026-10-14 this area lived inside tacit-internal's `apps/internal`, split by path from the staff session; it moved here so the client product and Tacit's workspace no longer share an app, a domain or a session.
- **`lib/client/session.ts`**: `resolveClient()` builds a per-request database client from the user's own WorkOS access token (`createUserClient`, role `authenticated`) and asks the database who that is (`current_actor_id()`), so the page and the policies agree by construction. Not bound yet means a first sign-in: `lib/client/bind.ts` matches the verified email to the actor an admin invited, inside the token's org (`@tacit-ats/core`'s `bindClientActor`). Nobody is created on sign-in. `requireClient()` is the throwing form, for actions.
- **Sign-ins that start at WorkOS.** Accepting a WorkOS invitation (or signing up from its email) runs AuthKit's hosted flow and then lands on `/client/callback` without the PKCE cookie our own sign-in sets, so AuthKit cannot verify it and refuses (`CallbackError` `missing_pkce_cookie` / `missing_auth_params` / `oauth_state_mismatch`). The account exists by then; the callback's `onError` (`lib/client/callback-error.ts`) answers with a "Your account is ready, continue" page linking to `/client`, which signs in from our side. Any other failure is a plain "Couldn't sign in, try again" page, never the error itself. A link, not a redirect, so a browser that drops the cookie cannot loop. (Until 2026-10-14 this was AuthKit's bare JSON error, the first thing an invited client saw.)
- **`lib/client/bind.ts` is the only file in the client area that may touch the service role**, and it returns a verdict, never the client.
- **`src/client-area.test.ts`** enforces the rules above from the source: the import walk (nothing reachable from `app/client` or `lib/client` may name a privileged client, except `lib/client/bind.ts`), and that every server action starts with `await requireClient()`.
- **Pages so far**: `/client` (Home: the Recruiting Overview below for admins and the hiring team; for a limited interviewer, the page it always was: the people they interview, the jobs they can see, the team), `/client/jobs` (the jobs you can see, with pipeline counts unless you are an interviewer) and `/client/jobs/[id]` (2026-10-13: the job and its pipeline on the shared `components/pipeline-board.tsx`, plus "Add a candidate"). Everything is read through RLS; nothing is filtered in the page. A refused sign-in renders `app/client/refusal.tsx` on every page.
- **The job board** reads with core's `getRolePipeline` on the member's own client, so the cards are exactly the applications they may read (an external recruiter sees only their own adds; an interviewer gets a pointer to `/client` instead of a board). The edit controls appear only when `can_on_role(job, 'can_edit_pipeline')` says so, and "Add a candidate" also needs `can_add_people()`. The writes (`jobs/[id]/actions.ts`: add, move, edit stage/owner/next action, log a touch, remove) are core's pipeline functions on the same client, so the policies decide again. Adding goes through core's `addCandidateToRole`: an email already on a person the member can see reuses that person, otherwise a people row is made in the org; a person in the org the member cannot see is not revealed, so they get a second row. The action checks `can_edit_pipeline` before creating anyone, so a refused add leaves no orphan person. Notices and errors are codes (`jobs/messages.ts`). Core never touches the Tacit funnel for a client org's application (`advancePersonFunnel` returns for any org but Tacit's), and `getRolePipeline`'s `companyName` is empty for a member who may not read `companies` (everyone but an admin). Cards do not link anywhere yet: there is no client person page.
- **Where a client org starts**: tacit-internal's ATS Admin (`/admin/orgs` in its `apps/internal`, staff admins only), with `@tacit/core`'s `createClientOrg` and `inviteOrgMember` on the service role. Nothing in this repo creates an org or its first admin; a first sign-in here only binds the actor that page invited.
- **`/client/team`** (2026-10-14): the org's members for everyone; an admin also sees who is invited and not signed in yet, and invites, re-sends and withdraws (`team/actions.ts`, codes in `team/messages.ts`). No service role: the actor is written by the `invite_org_member` definer function on the admin's own token (tacit-internal's packages/db, `20261014120000_client_invites`), which checks the caller is an admin and writes only into the org on that token, so the form never names an org. WorkOS then sends the email (`lib/client/workos.ts`, AuthKit's own `getWorkOS()` client, with the inviter named), and a first invitation WorkOS refuses is withdrawn again. Withdrawing deletes the unbound actor and revokes WorkOS's pending invitation for the address, best-effort: an accepted email with no actor behind it binds nobody. `@tacit-ats/core`'s `client-auth/invite.ts` holds the sequence. `ClientContext` now carries the token's WorkOS organization and user ids for this.
- **`/client/pipeline`** (2026-10-29; design/tats's Pipeline, the sidebar's **Routing & Tracking**): every open job's live applications on one read-only board. `lib/client/pipeline.ts`'s `readPipeline` reads on the member's own token: each open job through core's `getRolePipeline` (not `listOpenPipelines`, which reads `companies(name)`, admin-only for a client member, and fails for everyone else), and the non-cancelled `interviews` of the `interviewing` applications (150 ids a read). The columns are **computed, not stored** (`lib/client/pipeline-columns.ts`, pure, unit-tested): New (sourced, contacted), Screening, Scheduling (interviewing, nothing booked or held), Interview booked (a scheduled interview not yet ended), Your call (interviews held, a `scheduled` one past its end counting as held, nothing booked next), Offer; hired / rejected / withdrawn are left off. Job tabs (`?job=`), a name filter (`?q=`, a GET form), a dozen cards a column until "Show all" (`?all=<column>`). A card is the person, the job (the headline once one job is picked), one status line (relative times only, "Interview in 2d", so no time zone is needed) red when core calls it stalled, and the owner; it opens the job's board, where the edit controls are. An interviewer gets the same pointer home as on a job page. `pipeline.integration.test.ts` runs the read against the local stack: an admin's columns, a hiring manager seeing only their job (and its interviews), another company never.
- **`/client`, Home** (2026-10-29; design/tats's Recruiting Overview, "where are candidates getting stuck?"): for admins and the hiring team, with the period (`?days=7|30|90`, a `Segmented`) and the job (`?job=`, tabs of the open jobs) in the URL. `lib/client/home.ts`'s `readHome` reads on the member's own token: the Pipeline board's `readPipeline` for the live candidates and their computed columns, their `application_events` stage moves, `applications` added in the period and the one before, the non-cancelled `interviews` starting in the last and next period, and `@tacit-ats/core`'s `pipelineReport` and `interviewerActivity` (`clientTools`, a ctx made from the session). `lib/client/home-metrics.ts` is the arithmetic, pure and unit-tested: the period comparison, applications per day (a week) or per week (longer), relative to now so no time zone is needed; how long each candidate has waited (since the last move into their stage, or added; in "Your call" since their last interview ended); time in stage per board column (segments under a day / 1 to 3 days / over 3, median, longest); bottlenecks (not yet screened, waiting to be scheduled, waiting on your call, feedback outstanding, core's stalled), oldest first; interviewer hours; feedback on time. `app/client/home-props.ts` words it, `home-view.tsx` draws it (props only, CSS bars, no chart library, `font-pixel` numbers). Cards: new applications vs the period before, candidates in process by column, waiting for review, waiting to be scheduled, interviewer feedback on time (admins and whoever manages a job: `interviewerActivity` refuses everyone else, and the card is hidden; it has no job filter, so with a job picked it says "every job you manage"), interview workload by interviewer. Below: bottlenecks beside time in stage (each row a `<details>` listing the longest waits, linking to the job's board), feedback time by interviewer, and the average stay in each stage for applications added in the period (`pipelineReport`). Left out, with no data behind them: Testbench, user-set targets and alerts, interview efficiency (round-to-round pass rates, interviews per hire), the team filter (no teams), "Compare styles". `home.integration.test.ts` runs the read against the local stack: an admin's numbers on known fixtures, a job filter, a hiring manager counting only their job, a member on no job getting no feedback card, another company never.
- Not built yet: a client person page (cards are plain names), creating and editing jobs, changing a member's access level, files. The WorkOS and Supabase setup was done on 2026-10-13; production is `ats.tacitawareness.com`, where a test client org's admin first signed in on 2026-10-15.
- **`/client/calendar`** (2026-10-17): a member connects their own Google Calendar for the client connector's scheduling (apps/mcp `find_interview_times`, `schedule_interview`). `calendar/connect` (GET, `resolveClient()`) sets a 10-minute httpOnly state cookie bound to the actor and redirects to Google's consent (`lib/client/google-calendar.ts`: scopes openid, email, `calendar.events.freebusy`, `calendar.events`, offline, `prompt=consent`; `calendar.events.freebusy` since 2026-10-23, the scope Google verified, which reads the busy times of calendars the member can see, not only their own: grants made before then carry `calendar.freebusy`, which freeBusy also accepts); `calendar/callback` checks the state against the cookie, exchanges the code (refusing a grant without both calendar scopes, a refresh token or a verified account email), and stores it with `client_connect_calendar` on the member's own token, which writes only the caller's row (tacit-internal's packages/db, `20261017120000_client_calendar_connections`). The page reads the member's own row (never the refresh token, which `authenticated` is not granted); `calendar/actions.ts` disconnects through `client_disconnect_calendar`. Codes in `calendar/messages.ts`. Needs `CLIENT_GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID` / `CLIENT_GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET` and `<origin>/client/calendar/callback` registered with Google; unset, the page says it is not set up. Since 2026-10-19 its connect, callback and disconnect all return to `/client/integrations`, which shows its row, and `/client/calendar` itself only redirects there (kept for old links and the client connector's "connect it at" sentence).
- **`/client/integrations`** (2026-10-19): the sidebar's "Integrations", each member's own connections, each with a Connect that runs the provider's own sign-in and comes back here. **Granola**: `integrations/granola/connect` (GET, `resolveClient()`) registers a public OAuth client with Granola's MCP auth server for this connection (dynamic client registration, so nothing to configure: no client id, secret or env var), sets a 10-minute httpOnly cookie holding the actor, state, client id and PKCE verifier, and redirects to Granola's sign-in (`lib/client/granola.ts`: scopes openid, email, profile, offline_access, `resource=https://mcp.granola.ai/mcp`); `integrations/granola/callback` checks the state against the cookie and the actor, exchanges the code with the verifier (refusing a grant without a refresh token), and stores it with `client_connect_granola` on the member's own token, which writes only the caller's row (tacit-internal's packages/db, `20261019200000_client_granola_oauth`); the id token's email is the account shown. `integrations/actions.ts` disconnects through `client_disconnect_granola`. The page reads the member's own row and never a token, which `authenticated` is not granted. Codes in `integrations/messages.ts`; nothing secret rides in a URL. (The first version, earlier on 2026-10-19, asked for a pasted Granola API key.) **Google Calendar**: Connect runs `/client/calendar/connect` (its OAuth routes stay where Google has them registered). Each connected row offers Reconnect and Disconnect. Laid out like the Connectors card on Tacit's internal home (tacit-internal apps/internal `(workspace)/page.tsx`): one row per connector, app tile, name, one detail line, and Connect or a green Connected with its links. The markup is `integrations/view.tsx`, props only; `page.tsx` does the reads. Icons in `components/brand-icons.tsx` (`public/granola.png`). The client connector reads Granola for `get_interview_notes` (apps/mcp `client/granola.ts`, the caller's own grant only).
- **Tacit's submissions** (2026-10-17): Tacit submits candidates into a client's jobs like a recruiting agency (tacit-internal's packages/db, `20261017130000_tacit_submissions`, and its Workspace's `/people/[id]/submit`). Tacit recruits for any of a client's open jobs, so the client area has no setting for it: a `/client/settings` page that let an admin limit or turn off Tacit's access was removed on 2026-10-16. A submission lands on the job board as an ordinary card with an agency pill reading its `source_detail` ("Tacit · <teammate>"), added by that teammate's External Recruiter actor, with their note on the candidate. Those actors are never offered as invitations to re-send (`org_pending_invites` skips them).
- **`/client/docs`** (2026-10-14): the client connector's docs, linked from the sidebar as "Documentation", behind the same `resolveClient()` gate as the other client pages. The pages are data in `lib/docs/client-pages.ts`, rendered by `components/docs/docs-view.tsx` (react-markdown + remark-gfm) with a ⌘K search. `lib/docs/docs.test.ts` reads `apps/mcp/src/client/server.ts` and fails when the tool reference lists a tool that is not registered or misses one that is: **add a row to the reference page when you add a tool.** tacit-internal's staff `/docs` shows a copy of these pages (its `lib/docs/client-pages.ts`); this file is the source, so tell that repo when it changes. `CLIENT_CONNECTOR_URL` is `https://ats-mcp.tacitawareness.com/api/client`, the mcp app's production domain: it must equal that project's `CLIENT_MCP_RESOURCE_URL`, the token audience.
- **Public job posts** (`/jobs/[slug]`, `/api/jobs/[slug]/apply`, 2026-10-16): a client's live post and its application form, for anyone with the link. Not part of the client area and not behind any session: `proxy.ts` runs only under `/client` (pinned by `proxy.test.ts`'s matcher test). The only database access is `lib/public/jobs.ts`, on the publishable key with no token (`createUserClient` with a null token, which also refuses a secret key), so every call is `anon`, which may run `job_post_public` and `submit_job_application` and read nothing else (tacit-internal's packages/db, `20261016150000_client_job_posts`). A wrong slug, a draft, a closed post and a post on a job that is not open are the same 404. The page is server-rendered with no script: one input per question named `a_<question id>`, the browser's own `required` / `maxLength` / `type` checks first, and a hidden `website` honeypot the database answers with a quiet success. The form posts to the route, which calls the function and answers with a 303 back to the page carrying a code (`applied=1`, or `error=required|too_long|invalid|busy|failed` and `q=<question id>`), never the database's message; the page owns the wording. What was typed is not kept across an error redirect, which is why the browser checks matter. No resume upload yet: the resume question takes a link. `client-area.test.ts` walks the imports from `app/jobs`, `app/api/jobs` and `lib/public` and fails on a session, a privileged client or a `"use server"` file. The members' side (posting, publishing, the applicants inbox) is apps/mcp's client connector. Since 2026-10-16 the form ends with a **voluntary self-identification** fieldset (US EEO-1 gender and race / ethnicity, veteran and disability status; `EEOC_QUESTIONS` in `lib/public/jobs.ts`), apart from the post's questions, every select defaulting to "I decline to self-identify" and saying the hiring team never sees the answers; the fields are `eeoc_<question>`, passed as `submit_job_application`'s `p_eeoc` and stored with source `candidate` on the new application (tacit-internal's packages/db, `20261016170000_client_reports_eeoc`). Members only ever see an admin's suppressed totals (apps/mcp's `eeoc_report`).

- **Interview notifications to each company's own bot** (2026-10-28): the same machinery tacit-internal runs for Tacit's bot (its `@tacit/core` `notify/`: the Google Calendar sync, the exact five-minute reminder, the check-in after an interview, the cancellation notice), run here once per client company. **The bot** is a row in `agent_destinations` (tacit-internal's packages/db, `20261028120000_notify_per_company`), set by an admin on `/client/integrations` (`integrations/bot.tsx`, `bot-actions.ts`): the four admin-only definer functions on their own token set the https URL (the secret comes back once, as the action's result, shown on the page and never in a URL), rotate the secret, turn it on or off and remove it, each audited. **Verify** (`lib/client/bot.ts`, the second client-area module allowed a privileged client, after reading the row on the admin's own token) sends core's signed `url_verification` challenge; nothing is sent to a bot until it echoes one. **The notifier** is `lib/notify/notifier.ts`, outside the client area, on the service role: `companiesWithBots` (enabled and verified), `companyCalendar` (a member's `calendar_connections` grant with this app's Google client, core's `CalendarDeps.tokenFor`), `companyHub` (the bot as the only channel). `/api/cron/notify` (every two minutes, `vercel.json`, `CRON_SECRET`) runs `runNotifications` with each company's `orgId`; one company failing does not stop the next. `src/workflows/interview-reminder.ts` is the exact T-5 timer (Vercel Workflow; `withWorkflow` in `next.config.ts`; its routes under `/.well-known/workflow/`, outside the proxy's `/client` matcher), re-reading the company's bot when it wakes. `/api/agents/interview-thread` is a bot's thread callback: the Bearer secret names the company. The bot's contract is the `team-bot` docs page. Tests: `api/cron/notify/route.test.ts`, `api/agents/interview-thread/route.test.ts`; the core behaviour is tacit-internal's `per-company.integration.test.ts`. Not yet run against a real company's bot.

## Shared pieces

- **The palette is Hy's tATS prototype's** (2026-10-29; `design/tats`, the client dashboard this area is being ported from): `app/globals.css` holds its resolved tokens, light and dark (warm off-white, near-black in dark, one indigo `--accent` for the primary action and focus, `--green*` for ok / done, `--red` for broken or overdue, the `--space-1..6` 4-24px scale, plus `--pane`, `--sel`, `--st-*`, `--ev-*` for the pages still to come), Inter for text (`layout.tsx`), solid per-name `Avatar` colours (`avatarColor` in `page.tsx`) and a plain soft fill for the active nav item (`.nav-on`). The token names are the ones the components already used, so a value change in `globals.css` reaches every page. Follow `design/tats`, not tacit-internal, for colour and type.
- **The components are tacit-internal's** (2026-10-22): `lib/theme.ts` (the `tacit-theme` cookie the root layout renders as `<html data-theme>`), `components/page.tsx` (the page grammar: `Page`, `PageHeader`, `StatStrip`, `Section`, `Rows`/`Row`, `Pill`, `Chip`, `Avatar`, `Glyph`, `Live`, `Tabs`, ...), `components/ui/*`, `components/status-badge.tsx`, `components/pipeline-board.tsx`, `components/submit-button.tsx`, `components/copy-button.tsx` and `components/docs/*` started as copies of its `apps/internal` ones (its 2026-10 redesign: Geist Mono for ids, Departure Mono for big numbers from `app/fonts`). Until 2026-10-29 they also wore its green-black palette and Geist. `components/app-shell/shell-frame.tsx` is design/tats's sidebar (2026-10-29), for the pages that exist: the org (opens the menu), search and collapse buttons; Home; the funnel's sections (only **Top of Funnel** so far: Job Postings with the org's open jobs under it); at the bottom Team, Integrations, the member, and Documentation with the theme toggle and sign-out. No counts. A section appears once it has a page, and **each new page adds its own link** (and its `CommandItem`); never link a page that does not exist. Drag the edge to resize (200-380px, below 140 it collapses), `[` collapses it to a 60px icon rail, double-click resets; both live in the `tacit-sidebar` cookie (`lib/sidebar.ts`), read in `client/layout.tsx` so the first paint is right. ⌘K opens `command-menu.tsx`, the "Go to" half of design/tats's menu (pages and open jobs); a page that claims ⌘K for itself listens in the capture phase and calls `preventDefault` (the docs' search does), and the shell skips a handled event. `ListReader` in `page.tsx` is the list-and-reader page (Inbox, Applicant Tickets, Interviews): rendered as the page itself, not inside `Page`, it fills the panel edge to edge (`.shell-page:has(> .list-reader)` in `globals.css`). `client/layout.tsx` feeds the shell on the member's own token; `resolveClient()` is wrapped in React's `cache()` so the layout and the page share one resolution per request, and a refused sign-in gets no sidebar. What the ATS adds: `Notice` in `page.tsx` (a code's notice or error under the header) and the board's agency pill. Pages compose these instead of restating classes. They are not synced: to follow a later internal change, copy it across by hand. Until 2026-10-22 this app wore Tacit Testbench's skin (Neco / Recoleta, frosted glass) under a top header.
- Everything the pages read or write goes through `@tacit/core`'s pipeline functions (vendored, `vendor/`) or `@tacit-ats/core`, always on the member's own client.
